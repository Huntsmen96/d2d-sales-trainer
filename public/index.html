What Makes Every Door Different

Names, jobs, cities, moods, backstories, objections, bills, and household situations are all picked independently and fed into an AI that plays your customer in real time — so even two "skeptical nurses in Fort Worth" will respond completely differently based on their backstory and primary objection.

Scoring Philosophy

A Gracious Exit earns +40 pts. A burned lead costs −30 pts — because the next rep who knocks that door pays the price. Protecting the lead is a skill, not a retreat.
